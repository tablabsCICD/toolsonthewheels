About

Your trade, on the move

Tools on the Wheels is a GTA-based handyman and renovation service built on a simple idea: skilled trades shouldn't be a hassle to book. The truck arrives stocked, the job gets done with care, and the space is left cleaner than we found it.

Run hands-on by Anand Hunjan, every job is treated as if it were our own home. We work with you from the first phone call to the final walk-through — listening to what you actually need, giving straight advice, and finishing to a standard you'll be glad to live with.

Whether you're refreshing one room, completing a renovation, transforming a space, preparing turnover units, or finally tackling the small fixes that pile up, you get the same attention to detail — because the finish is what stays behind.

Good To Know

Frequently Asked

Do you offer free quotes?+
Yes. Every quote is free and comes with no obligation. Reach out with a description or a few photos, or book a consultation and we'll assess the job in person before giving you a clear, written estimate.
What areas do you cover?+
We're based in the Greater Toronto Area and travel across the region — including Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, Scarborough and beyond. If you're nearby and not listed, just ask.
Do you handle small jobs, or only big renovations?+
Both. No job is too small — from a single patch, a sticking door or a leaky faucet, all the way up to flooring a whole room or finishing a basement. If it's handyman or renovation work, it's fair game.
Do you supply materials, or do I?+
Either works. We can supply materials and roll the cost into your quote, or install materials you've already bought. We'll advise on quantities and quality so nothing gets wasted.
How do I book?+
Call or text +1 (514) 571-9041, email toolsonthewheels@gmail.com, or use the consultation form on this page. We'll confirm a time that works for you, including evenings and weekends.
How long will my project take?+
It depends on the scope. Small fixes are often same-visit, while larger jobs like flooring or cabinets may take a day or more. You'll get a realistic timeline as part of your quote.
